About the Studio

Panchromatic Studios is an independent team focused on blending high-fidelity technical concepts with engaging gameplay and strong narratives.

The name "Panchromatic" originates from black-and-white photographic film that is sensitive to all visible colors of the spectrum. We take a similar approach: capturing the full spectrum of art, code, and narrative, while remaining strictly "black and white" with our business model.

We believe games should be fun. A sense of accomplishment should come from playing the game, not from opening your wallet. Games are a multidisciplinary art form made by people. For us, AI is a tool in the creator's toolbox—used to help artists and developers achieve more, not to replace them.
